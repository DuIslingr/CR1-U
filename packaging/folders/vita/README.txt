Chaos Rings (CR1:U): your Vita copy goes here - the installed, decrypted PCSG00497 folder (CR1/resources/cr1.psarc, CR1/sound/ ...),
or what is in it.
An encrypted (NoNpDrm) dump has to be installed first, e.g. with Vita3K.

The iOS copy is the game; the Vita and Android ones add their voices, music, languages and
art to it (Options, Game). Alone, the Vita copy or the Android release is the game.
The README's "Your copy".
