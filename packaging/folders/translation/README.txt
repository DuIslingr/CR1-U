Chaos Rings (CR1:U): a fan translation goes here (optional) - metastigma's Russian one: its rePatch
folder (PCSG00497/, with CR1/resources/cr1.psarc in it) in a folder of its own, or the archive it
comes in (.7z, .rar, .zip), unpacked here into ru/ on the next start (the archive can then go).

It doesn't replace anything: the game's own files stay as they are, and the translation is a
language of its own after the game's eight - "Русский (fan translation)" in Options, Game, Language -
with English still there. Its text, and the art it redrew (the message window, the menus' names,
the titles), are read from it while it is the language. It works over the iOS copy, the Vita copy
or both; the Vita copy as the game shows all of its redrawn title screens.
The README's "Fan translation".
